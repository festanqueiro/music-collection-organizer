# 0054. A static website whose screenshots and clips are captured from the real app

## Context
MCO needs a marketing page with screenshots and short demo clips. Hand-made screenshots go stale
with every UI change, can't show a real collection (the user's is private), and recording clips
by hand on a Mac is slow to redo.

## Decision
- The site is plain HTML/CSS in `website/`, published by GitHub Pages through a workflow; no
  framework or build step.
- Its images and clips are generated by a script (`npm run site:capture`): a synthesized, made-up
  demo collection (fictional artists, ffmpeg-generated loops with a real tempo and key), a library
  set up through the app's own IPC API, Playwright driving the built Electron app with its own data
  folder, and ffmpeg recording a virtual X screen (Linux, xvfb) for the clips. The output is
  committed.
- The capture uses the real app — real analysis, waveforms and visualizer (software WebGL) — never
  mock-ups.

## Alternatives considered
- **Mock the renderer in a browser** (a fake `window.api`): no Electron needed, but the player,
  analysis and visualizer would be faked, and the mock drifts from the app.
- **Hand-made captures on a Mac**: authentic, but slow to redo and tied to one person's library.
- **Playwright's own video recording**: simpler, but lower quality than recording the screen.
- **A static-site generator**: unnecessary for one page.

## Consequences
- Updating the site's visuals is one command on Linux (a container or CI); macOS can redo the
  screenshots only.
- The demo collection's audio is synthetic loops, fine at a glance but not real music.
- `website/` is excluded from the app's package (`build.files`).
