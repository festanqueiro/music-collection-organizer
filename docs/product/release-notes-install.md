## Install

### Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

1. Download **`MCO-<version>-arm64.dmg`** below, open it, and drag **MCO**
   into **Applications**.
2. Open MCO. macOS says *"Apple could not verify 'MCO' is free of malware"*.
   Click **Done**. This is expected: MCO isn't registered with Apple's
   paid developer program.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the
   *"MCO was blocked…"* message, click **Open Anyway**, and confirm with
   your password.

You only need to do this once per Mac. Updates are installed the same
way: drag the new version into Applications and replace the old one.
Your library and settings are kept.

### Windows

For 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. New, and not yet tried on a Windows PC.

1. Download **`MCO-<version>-win-x64-setup.exe`** below and run it.
2. Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*. Click **More info**, then
   **Run anyway**. MCO isn't signed with a paid certificate.
3. Follow the installer.

MCO doesn't update itself on Windows yet: run the new version's installer
over the old one. Your library and settings are kept.
