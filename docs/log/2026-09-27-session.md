# Session, 2026-09-27: record mode polish, mic, visualizer frame rate, Cast tries

Release **1.0.47** (PR #84, with the record mode built earlier the same day). Decisions:
[ADR 0045](../adr/0045-tv-renders-3d-themes-at-30-fps.md).

## Shipped (on top of recording, the Mic and the Live screen)
- **Visualizer frame rate**: 30 fps by default, with a picker (15/24/30/60/Max). The cap went into
  threejs-visualisers itself (v0.2.0, PR #5 there: `fps` option, `FrameLimiter`, `FPS_CHOICES`, and
  frame-rate-independent smoothing/beat detection); the Cast receiver renders its 3D themes at 30 fps.
- **Record**: level meter (L/R, peak hold, clip light) and a Level knob after the record bus.
- **Mic**: player-bar Mic popover (on/off, input, level, Talk, Hear myself, Noise suppression); Pitch
  (granular shifter in the mic worklet); voice EQ bands moved; FX button lights for mic effects.
- **FX screen** in three groups: Music FX, Mic FX, Instruments (Dub Siren).
- **App menu** says MCO / MCO BETA (userData pinned before `app.setName`).
- **Cast**: the TV goes back to "Load a song to continue" when the queue empties.

## Tried and dropped
- **3D themes back in the Cast menu** (at 30 fps): dropped again — not worth it for the Chromecast HD.
- **Google TV remote control** of playback: keydown never reached the receiver, and loading tracks
  through CAF's `PlayerManager` left the TV on "Load a song". Reverted
  ([research](../research/cast-devices.md#google-tv-remote-dropped-2026-09-27)).
- **macOS Voice Isolation** for the mic: Chromium lists the constraint but doesn't apply it; the
  noise-suppression switch uses Chromium's WebRTC suppressor instead.

## Issues faced (symptom → cause → fix)
- **Blank BETA window after opening Rec.** The new record meter set `channelInterpretation` on a
  `ChannelSplitterNode`, which throws; the exception unmounted React. → mix to stereo with a gain
  before the splitter, and the meter can no longer throw into the UI.
- **Mic EQ "not working".** Its shelves were at 150 Hz / 6 kHz, where an AirPods hands-free mic
  (24 kHz, little above ~4–8 kHz) has almost nothing. → 250 Hz / 1 kHz / 3.5 kHz.
- **Media keys not reaching MCO.** macOS's Now Playing was Safari (Disney+), not MCO; not a bug.
- **FX button not lit with Pitch on.** Mic effects only counted while the mic was on → they count
  whenever switched on, like the music's.
- **The Cast receiver deploy from a branch** is refused by the `github-pages` environment (main only);
  testing a branch receiver meant adding the branch to the environment's deployment policy for one
  run, then removing it.

## Process notes
- Driving BETA over DevTools: launch with `--remote-debugging-port=9333`; `Page.captureScreenshot`
  and `Runtime.evaluate` (with `Runtime.exceptionThrown` on reload to catch a crash).
- The Chromecast HD's debugging port (9222) is closed, so the receiver can't be inspected there.
