Your music, organised.

MCO is a desktop app for DJs who keep their music as files. Point it at your folder: it works out every track's BPM, key and waveform, lets you tag it your own way, build playlists, mix harmonically, add effects and record — and takes it all to Rekordbox. Nothing leaves your machine.

Download for Mac See it in action
🔒

Local, and yours

Your music never gets uploaded, and MCO never rewrites your audio files behind your back. Tags and analysis live in MCO's own database, backed up daily.

🏷️

Your tags, your way

Two levels — Tags and Subtags — with colours, AND/OR filters and bulk tagging. Organise by vibe, set or venue, not just by genre.

🎧

Made for DJs

BPM, key in Camelot, energy and waveform for every track; a Compatible filter for harmonic mixing; headphone pre-listen; MIDI with LED feedback.

🔁

Plays well with Rekordbox

Bring your Rekordbox playlists in — even from old USB sticks — and send your tags and playlists back as Rekordbox XML or m3u8.

Tags & search

Find the right track in seconds

Tick a Tag or two, switch between any and all, then type to narrow it down. Every Tag and Subtag has its own colour, so a list reads at a glance.

  • Tags and Subtags, filterable with OR / AND
  • Search across title, artist, album and tags
  • Tag one track or a hundred at once, with undo

Playlists

Build sets, keep them in folders

Check a few songs and add them to a playlist, drag rows to put them in order, and play the whole thing — or a whole folder of playlists. ⌫ takes a song out, with Undo.

  • Playlists in folders, in a box under the sidebar
  • Drag to reorder; Play playlist replaces the queue (with Undo)
  • Import from Rekordbox (XML, m3u8 or text) and export back
  • Old USB-stick playlists: songs found in your collection even when the paths are wrong

Harmonic mixing

Only what mixes with what's playing

Every key is shown on the Camelot wheel. Turn on Compatible and the list shrinks to the tracks that mix with the one playing: the same key, one step round the wheel, or the relative major/minor — at a tempo that matches.

  • BPM, key, energy and waveform, analysed on your Mac
  • Camelot or classic key notation
  • Pre-listen in your headphones on a second output

Visualizer

Put the music on the big screen

Nine audio-reactive themes, full screen — on your Mac, on a second display or projector, or cast to a TV with MCO's own Cast app showing what's playing and what's next.

  • Nebula, Warp, Horizon, Sound System, Smoke and more
  • Show on a screen (Apple TV, projector) with a visual delay
  • Cast to Chromecast, Google TV or a Nest speaker

Effects, mic & recording

Run a show — or a podcast

Rotary-knob effects on the music, your voice through its own chain, a dub siren on top, and everything recorded to WAV, FLAC or MP3. The Live screen puts the queue and every effect on one page.

  • EQ, filter, tempo-synced delay, reverb
  • Mic with noise gate, compressor, pitch, echo throws, radio voice and ducking
  • Map any knob or button to your MIDI controller in two clicks

Stats

Your collection, in numbers

How much you have, how good the files are, your tempos and keys round the wheel, your top tags and artists, and what you added when — for everything or one folder.

A closer look

Everything in the box

Library

WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, M4A/AAC, OGG/Opus. Watches your folder for new downloads. Missing files are hidden, not forgotten. Google Drive cloud-only files download when played.

Analysis

BPM, key, loudness, a 1–10 energy rating and the waveform — in the background, while you keep working.

File tags

Edit title, artist, album, genre and year inside the file, byte for byte; suggestions from the filename, never saved without you.

Queue & player

Play now, play next, add to queue; shuffle; continuous play; a clickable waveform, CDJ-style cue, media keys and output choice.

Filters

Compatible, analysed, duplicates, untagged, missing metadata, missing files — combined with folders and tags.

Rekordbox

Export tags and playlists as Rekordbox XML or m3u8; import playlists from XML, m3u8 or text, and refresh them later.

MIDI

Learn any knob, toggle or transport button; LED feedback on controllers that support it.

Themes

Three dark and three light colour themes.

Backups & updates

Daily backups with one-click restore, a copy to an external disk, and one-click updates.

Install

  1. Download the latest MCO-…-arm64.dmg from the releases page, open it and drag MCO into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS says it “could not verify” MCO — it isn't registered with Apple's paid developer programme. Click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Pick your music folder. From then on MCO updates itself with one click.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). A Windows installer is published with every release but hasn't been tested on a Windows PC yet.
